### M1-Q23
SKILL: Transitions | RELATION: result | DIFFICULTY: Easy | TOPIC: Science — plant biology | WORDS: 48
MENU-RELATIONS: exemplification / result / concession-reversal / restatement
PASSAGE:
Leaf pores admit the carbon dioxide that plants need for photosynthesis, but they also let water vapor escape, a costly exchange in the midday heat of a North American desert. ______ agaves and other succulents there keep their pores tightly sealed all day and open them only after dark.
PROMPT: Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?
A) For example,
B) As a result,
C) Nevertheless,
D) In other words,
CORRECT: B
RATIONALE:
Choice B is the best answer. "As a result," logically signals that the behavior described in this sentence is a consequence of the constraint described in the previous sentence: because open pores lose water in desert heat, agaves keep their pores closed until the cooler hours after sunset.
Choice A is incorrect because "For example," illogically signals that the information in this sentence is one instance of the exchange described in the previous sentence. Instead, the sealed pores of agaves are a response to that exchange.
Choice C is incorrect because "Nevertheless," illogically signals that the information in this sentence occurs despite the water loss described in the previous sentence. Instead, the water loss is precisely what accounts for it.
Choice D is incorrect because "In other words," illogically signals that this sentence restates the previous sentence in different terms. Instead, it introduces new information about how certain desert plants behave.

### M1-Q24
SKILL: Transitions | RELATION: contrast (adversative) | DIFFICULTY: Medium | TOPIC: Arts — dance | WORDS: 55
MENU-RELATIONS: exemplification / result / addition / contrast
PASSAGE:
Dance historian Miriam Adeyemi notes that Labanotation, a symbolic system for recording human movement on a staff, was designed to give choreography the same permanence that written scores have long given music. ______ few companies actually commission notated scores; they rely on video recordings and on the memories of the dancers who first learned the choreography.
PROMPT: Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?
A) For instance,
B) As a result,
C) In addition,
D) However,
CORRECT: D
RATIONALE:
Choice D is the best answer. "However," logically signals that the information in this sentence is inconsistent with the previous information: although Labanotation was created to preserve choreography permanently, most companies do not use it and depend on video and memory instead.
Choice A is incorrect because "For instance," illogically signals that the practice described in this sentence is an example of the purpose described in the previous sentence. Instead, it runs counter to that purpose.
Choice B is incorrect because "As a result," illogically signals that companies avoid notated scores because Labanotation was designed to make choreography permanent. Instead, no such causal relationship exists between the two claims.
Choice C is incorrect because "In addition," illogically signals that the information in this sentence supplements the previous information. Instead, the information conflicts with it.

### M1-Q25
SKILL: Rhetorical Synthesis | GOAL-FAMILY: introduce to an audience unfamiliar with X | DIFFICULTY: Medium | TOPIC: Arts — printmaking | BULLETS: 5 | NOTES-WORDS: 67
PASSAGE:
While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:
- Verónica Salgado is a printmaker who lives and works in Oaxaca, Mexico.
- Salgado works in chine-collé, or the bonding of thin sheets of paper onto a heavier sheet during printing.
- She completed *Cuenca*, a series of twelve chine-collé prints, in 2019.
- Salgado dyes the thin sheets with pigments she makes from local plants.
- Each of the prints in the series depicts a river in the Oaxacan highlands.

The student wants to introduce the series *Cuenca* to an audience unfamiliar with chine-collé.
PROMPT: Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?
A) Salgado, who lives and works in Oaxaca, dyes the thin sheets for her prints with pigments she makes from local plants.
B) Salgado works in chine-collé, a technique in which thin sheets of paper are bonded onto a heavier sheet during printing.
C) Salgado completed *Cuenca*, a series of twelve chine-collé prints, in 2019, and each one depicts a river in the Oaxacan highlands.
D) In her 2019 series *Cuenca*, Salgado uses chine-collé, a technique in which thin sheets of paper are bonded onto a heavier sheet.
CORRECT: D
RATIONALE:
Choice D is the best answer. The sentence introduces the series by naming it and dating it, and it assumes that the audience is unfamiliar with chine-collé, explaining what the technique involves.
Choice A is incorrect. While the sentence accurately describes where Salgado works and how she prepares her materials, it never mentions *Cuenca*; it doesn't introduce the series to an audience unfamiliar with chine-collé.
Choice B is incorrect. The sentence explains what chine-collé involves for an audience unfamiliar with it, but it says nothing about *Cuenca*; it doesn't introduce the series.
Choice C is incorrect. The sentence introduces the series but treats chine-collé as a term the audience already knows; it doesn't accommodate an audience unfamiliar with the technique.

- Halloran found that most positions in her sample lasted fewer than eighteen months.
- Wages tended to rise slightly each time a worker moved to a new household.

### M2-Q22

PASSAGE:
Ecologist Tuan Pham points out that a freshwater marsh performs several services for the landscape around it, few of which are visible to a visitor standing on the shore. ______ the tangled roots of marsh grasses trap suspended sediment that would otherwise silt up the shipping channels far downstream.
PROMPT: Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?
A) For example,
B) Nevertheless,
C) In other words,
D) As a result,
CORRECT: A
RATIONALE:
Choice A is the best answer. "For example," logically signals that the sediment trapping described in this sentence is one of the several marsh services mentioned in the previous sentence.

Choice C is incorrect because "In other words," illogically signals that this sentence restates the previous claim in different terms. Instead, it supplies a specific instance of the services that claim refers to.
Choice D is incorrect because "As a result," illogically signals that marsh roots trap sediment because the marsh's services are hard to see. Instead, the invisibility of those services does not cause them.

### M2-Q23

PASSAGE:
Traditional cel animation depended on a division of labor. First, an animator sketched each pose in pencil on paper, and a second artist traced those sketches in ink onto clear plastic sheets. ______ a painter filled in the colors on the back of each sheet, leaving the inked lines clean on the front.
PROMPT: Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?
A) For example,
B) By contrast,
C) Next,
D) Likewise,
CORRECT: C
RATIONALE:
Choice C is the best answer. "Next," logically signals that the painting described in this sentence is the step that followed the sketching and inking described in the previous sentence, which begins the ordered account with "First."

Choice B is incorrect because "By contrast," illogically signals that the painting stage differs from the previous stages in some meaningful way. Instead, it simply follows them.
Choice D is incorrect because "Likewise," illogically signals that the painting stage resembles the previous stages. Instead, the text presents the stages as consecutive rather than comparable.

### M2-Q24

MENU-RELATIONS: exemplification / emphasis / concession-reversal / result

Light is the only material a distant star will ever send to Earth. Astronomer Layla Haddad tells her students that a careful reading of that light gives up an unexpected amount of information about its source; ______ a single spectrum can disclose a star's chemical composition, its surface temperature, its motion relative to Earth, and the rate at which it spins.
PROMPT: Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?
A) for instance,
B) indeed,
C) nevertheless,
D) therefore,
CORRECT: B
RATIONALE:
Choice B is the best answer. "indeed," logically signals that the list in this clause is a stronger, more striking statement of the claim made in the previous clause: the amount of information available from starlight is unexpected because one spectrum yields four different properties of a star.
Choice A is incorrect because "for instance," illogically signals that this clause offers one member of a class of things named in the previous clause. Instead, it restates the previous claim in a more emphatic form.
Choice C is incorrect because "nevertheless," illogically signals that this clause holds despite the previous claim. Instead, it reinforces that claim.
Choice D is incorrect because "therefore," illogically signals that the properties listed in this clause follow as a consequence from the previous claim. Instead, they are what makes the claim striking.

- Pomo weavers in California build their baskets from sedge root and willow shoots.
- Some Pomo baskets are covered with woodpecker feathers and hung with clamshell beads.

While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:
- Most large desalination plants remove the salt from seawater by reverse osmosis.
- Reverse osmosis depends on a semipermeable membrane, or a barrier that water molecules can cross but salt cannot.
- Powerful pumps force seawater against one side of the membrane at high pressure.
- Fresh water collects on the other side and is piped away for use.
- The salty brine left behind is usually returned to the sea.

The student wants to explain how reverse osmosis works to an audience unfamiliar with the technology.
PROMPT: Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?
A) In reverse osmosis, pumps force seawater against one side of a semipermeable membrane, and fresh water collects on the other side.
B) Most large desalination plants remove the salt from seawater by reverse osmosis, and the leftover brine is usually returned to the sea.
C) In a desalination plant, a semipermeable membrane is a barrier that water molecules can cross but that salt cannot.

Choice D is the best answer. The sentence describes the mechanism of reverse osmosis and, for an audience unfamiliar with the technology, explains what a semipermeable membrane is and why it separates fresh water from salt.
