PROMPT: Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?
A) classifies
B) tracks
C) obscures
D) amplifies
CORRECT: B
RATIONALE:
Choice B is the best answer because it most logically completes the text. In this context, "tracks" means follows and records something as it changes over time. The colon introduces a description of exactly what Raghunathan does: a telescope "watches a single star for weeks, recording the tiny dips" in its brightness that reveal an orbiting planet.
Choice A is incorrect because there's nothing in the text to suggest that Raghunathan "classifies," or sorts into categories, the brightness of stars. Instead, the text describes a telescope following a single star's brightness over several weeks.
Choice C is incorrect because "obscures" means hides from view, which wouldn't make sense in context. The text indicates that Raghunathan is trying to observe starlight as closely as possible, not to block it.
Choice D is incorrect because although telescopes are often described as gathering light, there's no indication that Raghunathan "amplifies," or increases the strength of, the brightness of stars. The text says only that a telescope records small changes in that brightness.
