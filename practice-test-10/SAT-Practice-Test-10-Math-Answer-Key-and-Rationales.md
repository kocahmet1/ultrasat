# SAT Practice Test 10 — Math Answer Key and Rationales

## Math Module 1

### 1. A

_easy · linear-equations-two-variables_

Choice A is correct. The phrase “17 less than 4 times its y-coordinate” means subtract 17 from 4y, so x = 4y - 17. Choice B is incorrect because it adds 17 instead of subtracting 17. Choices C and D are incorrect because they make y, rather than x, the quantity described as 17 less than or greater than 4 times the other coordinate.

### 2. C

_easy · equivalent-expressions_

Choice C is correct. Distributing gives 2x² + 10x - (x² - x). Subtracting the second quantity changes both of its signs, so the result is 2x² + 10x - x² + x = x² + 11x. Choice A does not change the sign of the -x term inside the second product. Choice B omits the +x produced when x(x - 1) is subtracted. Choice D combines the x² terms by addition rather than subtraction.

### 3. B

_easy · one-variable-data_

Choice B is correct. The least number of recalibration attempts shown is 1, and the greatest is 6. The range is 6 - 1 = 5. Choice A is incorrect because 1 is the minimum data value, not the range. Choice C is incorrect because 6 is the maximum data value, not the range. Choice D is incorrect because 20 is the total number of gauges represented.

### 4. D

_easy · linear-equations-one-variable_

Choice D is correct. Distributing 7 gives 7x - 14 = 3x + 18. Subtracting 3x from both sides and adding 14 to both sides gives 4x = 32, so x = 8. Choices A, B, and C are incorrect because substituting 2, 4, or 6 for x does not make the two sides of the equation equal.

### 5. 88

_easy · area-volume_

The correct answer is 88. The area A of a triangle is one-half the product of its base b and perpendicular height h, so A = (1/2)bh. Substituting b = 22 and h = 8 gives A = (1/2)(22)(8) = 88 square inches. Adding the side lengths or using 22(8) without the factor of one-half would not give the area of the triangular panel.

### 6. 60

_easy · linear-equations-one-variable_

The correct answer is 60. Subtracting 7 from both sides gives (3/5)x = 12. Multiplying both sides by 5 gives 3x = 60. This is the quantity requested, so it is not necessary to divide by 3. Equivalently, solving completely gives x = 20, and then 3x = 3(20) = 60. Reporting 20 would give x rather than the requested value of 3x.

### 7. B

_easy · nonlinear-functions_

Choice B is correct. The graph opens upward, is symmetric about the y-axis, and has vertex (0, 1). These features agree with f(x) = x² + 1. Choice A is incorrect because its vertex is (0, -1). Choice C is incorrect because its graph opens downward. Choice D is incorrect because its vertex is (1, 0).

### 8. A

_easy · systems-linear-equations_

Choice A is correct. For (3, 2), the first equation gives 3 + 2(2) = 7 and the second gives 3(3) - 2 = 7, so the pair satisfies both equations. Choice B is incorrect because it reverses the coordinates of the solution. Choice C satisfies the first equation but not the second. Choice D satisfies the second equation but not the first.

### 9. D

_easy · ratios-rates-proportions_

Choice D is correct. Dividing 12.6 minutes by 7 paths gives 1.8 minutes per path. At this rate, 15 paths take 15(1.8) = 27 minutes. Choice A is the time for one path. Choice B is the time for the original 7 paths. Choice C doubles the original time, which accounts for 14 paths at the constant rate rather than the requested 15 paths.

### 10. C

_medium · nonlinear-equations_

Choice C is correct. If 3x - 5 is nonnegative, then 3x - 5 = x + 7, which gives x = 6. If 3x - 5 is negative, then -(3x - 5) = x + 7, which gives -3x + 5 = x + 7 and x = -1/2. Both values satisfy the original equation. Choice A uses the sum of the solutions as a solution. Choice B uses the product of the solutions as a solution. Choice D changes the sign of the negative solution.

### 11. A

_medium · inference-statistics_

Choice A is correct. When random samples are taken in the same way from the same population, a larger sample generally produces less sampling variability and a smaller margin of error. Choice B reverses this relationship. Choice C is incorrect because sample size affects margin of error even when the population is the same. Choice D is incorrect because a random sample estimate can still differ from the exact population proportion.

### 12. 11

_medium · equivalent-expressions_

The correct answer is 11. Multiplying both sides by 2x + 5 gives 2x² + kx + 15 = (2x + 5)(x + 3). Expanding the right side gives 2x² + 11x + 15. Since the equality holds for every allowed value of x, corresponding coefficients must be equal, so k = 11. With this value, the numerator factors as (2x + 5)(x + 3), confirming the quotient for x ≠ -5/2.

### 13. 5/2

_medium · two-variable-data_

The correct answer is 5/2. The two open, marked points on the line of best fit are (2, 8) and (8, 23). Slope is the change in the vertical coordinate divided by the corresponding change in the horizontal coordinate. Therefore, the slope is (23 - 8)/(8 - 2) = 15/6 = 5/2, which is also 2.5. Using individual scatterplot points instead of two points on the fitted line would not determine the displayed line’s exact slope.

### 14. D

_medium · linear-equations-two-variables_

Choice D is correct. Substituting x = 3 and y = -1 gives 3(k + 2) - 3(-1) = 18. Thus, 3k + 9 = 18, so 3k = 9 and k = 3. Choice A makes the left side 0, Choice B makes it 6, and Choice C makes it 12. None equals the required right side of 18.

### 15. C

_medium · lines-angles-triangles_

Choice C is correct. An exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two nonadjacent interior angles. Therefore, 7x - 5 = (3x + 5) + (2x + 10). Solving gives 2x = 20, so x = 10. The exterior angle is 7(10) - 5 = 65°. Choice A is the value of x, not the requested angle. Choice B results from omitting the +5 in the expression 3x + 5 when adding the two remote interior angles after finding x. Choice D is the adjacent interior angle, which is supplementary to the 65° exterior angle.

### 16. B

_medium · nonlinear-functions_

Choice B is correct. To use the known value f(2) = -5, set x - 4 = 2, which gives x = 6. Then g(6) = f(2) + 3 = -5 + 3 = -2, so (6, -2) lies on the graph of g. Choices A and C reverse the horizontal shift. Choices C and D subtract 3 from the output instead of adding 3.

### 17. D

_hard · circles_

Choice D is correct. Completing the square gives (x - 4)² + (y + 3)² = 25, so the center is (4, -3). The slope of the radius from (4, -3) to (8, 0) is 3/4, so the tangent slope is the negative reciprocal, -4/3. A line with this slope through (8, 0) is y = (-4/3)(x - 8) = (-4/3)x + 32/3. Therefore, b = 32/3. Choice A is the tangent slope, Choice B is the radius slope, and Choice C is the x-coordinate of the tangency point.

### 18. B

_hard · linear-functions_

Choice B is correct. The t-values are equally spaced, so the changes in T must be equal: (k - 5) - (3k + 7) = (-2k + 13) - (k - 5). This gives -2k - 12 = -3k + 18, so k = 30. Thus, T(2) = 97 and T(8) = 25, giving slope (25 - 97)/(8 - 2) = -12. Since 5 is 3 units after 2, T(5) = 97 + 3(-12) = 61. Choice A is T(2), Choice C is k, and Choice D is the slope.

### 19. -3/8

_hard · systems-linear-equations_

The correct answer is -3/8. Adding the equations eliminates y and gives 8x = 14, so x = 7/4. Substituting into 3x + 2y = 8 gives 21/4 + 2y = 32/4, so 2y = 11/4 and y = 11/8. Therefore, y - x = 11/8 - 14/8 = -3/8, which is -0.375. The order in the requested difference matters: x - y would have the opposite sign.

### 20. A

_hard · one-variable-data_

Choice A is correct. Multiplying every value by 3 and then subtracting 2 changes the mean to 3(14) - 2 = 40. Multiplying by 3 also multiplies the standard deviation by 3, but subtracting the same constant from every value does not change the spread, so the new standard deviation is 3s. Choices B and D incorrectly subtract 2 from the standard deviation. Choices C and D omit the subtraction of 2 when transforming the mean.

### 21. C

_hard · nonlinear-functions_

Choice C is correct. Equal output values of a quadratic occur at inputs equidistant from its axis of symmetry. Since 2 and 14 have midpoint (2 + 14)/2 = 8, statement I must be true. The inputs 5 and 11 are each 3 units from 8, so f(5) = f(11), and statement II must also be true. Choices A and B omit one statement that must be true, and Choice D omits both.

### 22. 17

_hard · nonlinear-equations_

The correct answer is 17. Let u = √(x + 18), so u is nonnegative and x = u² - 18. Substitution gives u² - u + (a - 18) = 0. If a is at most 17, the product of the two roots is negative, so exactly one root is nonnegative and yields one real x. At a = 18, the roots are 0 and 1, yielding two solutions. For positive integers a greater than 18, there are no nonnegative roots; in particular, the discriminant is already negative at a = 19. Therefore, the greatest possible value is 17.

## Math Module 2

### 1. B

_easy · ratios-rates-proportions_

### 2. D

### 3. A

### 4. C

Choice C is correct. Dividing the coefficients gives 18/6 = 3. For like bases, subtracting exponents gives x^(7-2) = x⁵ and y^(3-1) = y². Thus, the quotient is 3x⁵y². ### 5. 5

_easy · systems-linear-equations_

The correct answer is 5. Subtracting the second equation from the first eliminates the identical 2x terms: (2x + y) - (2x - y) = 17 - 7. This simplifies to 2y = 10, so y = 5. ### 6. 37.5

_easy · percentages_

The correct answer is 37.5. Fifteen percent is 15/100, or 0.15. Multiplying the total sample mass by this proportion gives 0.15(250) = 37.5 grams. The value 37.5 is equivalent to 75/2. Subtracting 15 from 250 or using 15 as a decimal multiplier would not represent 15% of the sample’s mass.

### 8. B

### 9. C

### 10. C

### 11. B

### 12. 7/2

_medium · nonlinear-equations_

The correct answer is 7/2. Factoring the quadratic gives (2x - 7)(x + 2) = 0 because the product expands to 2x² - 3x - 14. By the zero-product property, 2x - 7 = 0 or x + 2 = 0. Therefore, the solutions are x = 7/2 and x = -2. The question asks for the positive solution, so the answer is 7/2, which is 3.5.

### 13. 10

### 14. A

### 19. 2

### 22. 32

_hard · nonlinear-functions_

The correct answer is 32. Since the zeros are -1 and 7, f(x) = a(x + 1)(x - 7). Using f(2) = 30 gives 30 = a(3)(-5), so a = -2. The axis of symmetry is halfway between the zeros, at x = 3. Because a is negative, the parabola opens downward and the vertex gives its maximum. Thus, f(3) = -2(4)(-4) = 32. This value is greater than the given value f(2) = 30, as expected near the vertex.
