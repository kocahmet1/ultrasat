Singular vs. plural possessives — the 60‑second version

Why now: twice today (and twice on Jun 30) you placed the apostrophe before the ‑s on a plural noun. This is one small rule, and it keeps costing you points in Boundaries — currently one of your two focus areas.

The rule

Form the noun first. Then add the possessive. If the plural already ends in ‑s, the apostrophe goes after it.

the student's book
one student → student + 's
the students' books
many students → students + '

Your sentence, fixed

You chose "the three visitor's badges." Three visitors → plural first (visitors), then the apostrophe: "the three visitors' badges."
