## QC provenance

This form was authored to the blueprint above and then put through three independent adversarial
rounds before shipping: a per-band verifier over the 44 drafted items, a repair round plus fresh
per-band re-verification, and a final pass combining per-band regression checks with two whole-form
critics (one reading the 44 items cold for texture and pacing, one recomputing every distribution
against the measured base). Every finding was reproduced numerically before it was acted on.

The rounds changed 22 of the 44 items. The substantive catches, in order of severity:

1. **M3 Q7's key was reachable by the misread it tests.** With the drafted table, "more than 15 eggs"
   summed to 6 — which was also the printed frequency of the 15-egg row, so a student who read the
   threshold row itself scored correct. The 15-row frequency was changed to 7.
2. **Five firewall breaches against shipped or official material**: M3 Q4 (PT4 M4 Q3's parallel-lines
   item, similarity 0.96), M3 Q20 (official PT7 M1 Q26 and bank item 097e10f5 own the

   `4x + 19`), M4 Q10 (bank item 28ff3966 owns scatter-vs-elevation in a mountain setting), M4 Q20
   (word-for-word PT4 M3 Q22). Four more were caught in the first round: M3 Q5, M3 Q14, M4 Q7, M4 Q12.
3. **Two items mirrored a sibling's slot exactly**: M4 Q11 shared PT5 M4 Q11's slot, skill, difficulty,
   figure, stem template and key letter; M3 Q9 reused PT5 M3 Q8's four-option lattice.
4. **M3 Q18 did not earn its hard label** (one substitution at slot 18) and was rebuilt as a
