# Practice Test 10 - Math Modules 3 and 4: 44-Slot Blueprint

This blueprint is based on the eight complete Math modules in the supplied Practice Tests 4-7
(216 questions), the four supplied 100-question Question Bank exports, and the local Tests 1-6
and 9 originality corpus. Source measurements are treated as ranges, not templates to copy.
Practice Test 10 changes only overall Modules 3 and 4; Reading and Writing Modules 1 and 2 are
outside the replacement scope.

## Form architecture

| Dimension | Test 10 target | Source rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Questions | 22 + 22 | Digital SAT application shape |
| Time | 35 minutes per module | Current Digital SAT Math timing |
| Formats | 16 MC + 6 SPR per module | 32/12 whole-form split |
| SPR positions | 5, 6, 12, 13, 19, 22 | Interspersed pairs and one hard closer |
| Difficulty | 9 E / 7 M / 6 H per module | 18/14/12 whole-form distribution |
| Domains | ALG 14 / ADV 14 / PSDA 9 / GEO 7 | Source-centered domain rotation |
| Visuals | M3: 4, M4: 5 | 9/44 = 20.5% |
| Applied contexts | M3: 6, M4: 8 | 14/44 = 31.8% |

Both modules use the source-shaped contiguous ramp: questions 1-9 are easy, 10-16 are medium,
and 17-22 are hard. Module 4 is parallel rather than uniformly harder. The hard tier emphasizes
representation, constraints, invariants, and multi-stage setup; arithmetic stays controlled after
the governing idea is recognized.

## Auditable texture quotas

Applied items are M3 Q3, Q5, Q9, Q11, Q13, and Q18, and M4 Q1, Q2, Q6, Q7,
Q9, Q10, Q15, and Q16. These contexts carry a mathematical role rather than merely decorating
an abstract exercise.

The 12 SPR targets are:

- Integers: M3 Q5 = 88, Q6 = 60, Q12 = 11, Q22 = 17; M4 Q5 = 5,
  Q13 = 10, Q19 = 2, Q22 = 32.
- Fractions: M3 Q13 = 5/2 and Q19 = -3/8; M4 Q12 = 7/2.
- Natural decimal: M4 Q6 = 37.5.

The form distributes its traps: target-versus-intermediate value, variable-role reversal, a missed
sign change, unit cancellation, sample-size versus certainty, raw point versus fitted line, output
translation direction, radius slope versus tangent slope, mean versus spread, inclusive inequality
boundaries, compounding versus doubling a percent, predicted value versus residual, radius versus radius squared, and included
versus nonincluded angle information.

## Authenticity and originality controls

- All questions use new wording and numerical surfaces. No source question is copied.
- Hard questions require a structural recognition or two linked decisions, not merely larger numbers.
- Every MC distractor corresponds to a named adjacent quantity, reversal, omission, sign error, or
  representation error, and every rationale diagnoses all three distractors.
- Numeric choices are monotonic whenever ordering is meaningful.
- The nine visual surfaces comprise a bar graph, two function graphs/tables, two scatterplots, two
  geometry figures, a contextual line graph, and an inequality-region graph.
- No personal names or Latin binomials are used. Contexts avoid the repeated names, species, and
  heavily reused fee/rental, mixture-total, direct-cylinder, and survey-household vehicles in the
  local corpus.
- The validator compares normalized question surfaces against Tests 1-6 and 9 and binds every SVG
  filename to its exact module and question address.
- Replacement is limited to Math Modules 3 and 4. Old Math questions are backed up and soft-retired,
  never deleted; Reading and Writing Modules 1 and 2 are transactionally snapshotted and verified
  unchanged.
