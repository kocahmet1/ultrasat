## Cross-form constraints (validator-enforced — read carefully)

**Graphics**
- Exactly 4 graphic items, all on command-of-evidence: **M1Q10 `<table>` · M1Q11 SVG bar · M2Q10 SVG line · M2Q11 `<table>`**.
- Graphics are forbidden on every other item.
- Tables: `<table><caption>…</caption><thead><tr><th>…</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>…</td></tr></tbody></table>`, with a prose sentence naming units and years. Cell count must survive DOMPurify unchanged.
- SVGs: `<svg role="img" viewBox="0 0 W H" data-graph-type="bar|line"><title>…</title><desc>…</desc>…</svg>` with visible axis labels and tick values as `<text>` elements. Must survive DOMPurify with `viewBox`, `role="img"`, nonempty `<title>` and `<desc>` intact. Use only `rect`, `line`, `polyline`, `circle`, `text`, `g`, `path`.

**Blanks** — `______` (6+ underscores), exactly one, required for: words-in-context, inferences, boundaries, form-structure-sense, transitions, and the quantitative command-of-evidence items (M1Q10, M1Q11, M2Q10, M2Q11). **Forbidden** in every other item. The quotation COE items (M1Q13, M2Q12) may have at most one blank — prefer zero.

**Underline markers** — `[UNDERLINED]…[/UNDERLINED]` permitted **only** on M1Q6, M2Q6, and M2Q7. Exactly one nonempty span.

**Transitions** — 6 items, 6 distinct keyed words, 6 distinct relationships:
`Specifically`(example) · `Nevertheless`(contrast-concession) · `Instead`(replacement) · `Accordingly`(cause-result) · `Furthermore`(addition) · `Taken together`(synthesis).
Within each item the four options must express **four different relationships** — never two contrast words in one set.

**Boundaries** — 8 distinct conventions, none repeated inside a module. Options must be the *same words* with only punctuation varying.

**Form-structure-sense** — 7 items across 6 conventions (subject-verb agreement appears twice, once per module, in different flavors).

**Rhetorical synthesis** — passage begins exactly `While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:` followed by 5–7 `•` bullets. The stem sentence (`The student wants to …`) goes in the `text` field along with `Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?`

**Cross-text** — passage contains the literal strings `Text 1` and `Text 2`, ~60–80 words each.

**Prose stimulus word count** (excludes table/SVG markup): ≥ skill floor and ≤ 150.
`WIC 50 · TSP 75 · CTC 100 · CID 70 · COE 65 · INF 65 · BND 35 · FSS 35 · TRN 45 · RS 70`

**Explanations** — must begin literally `Choice {KEY}` and run 150–320 words, one plain-text paragraph.

**Option length parity** — for the long-option skills (TSP, CID, COE, INF, CTC, RS) all four options must be within ~30% of one another in word count. The keyed option must NOT be the uniquely longest option in more than half of a module's long-option items.
