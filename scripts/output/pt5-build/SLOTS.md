- Graphics: M1Q10 table · M1Q11 line · M2Q10 bar · M2Q11 table → 4 graphic items, all three types present.
  Graphics may appear **only** on command-of-evidence items.

- Boundaries: 8 distinct conventions, none repeated within a module.
- FSS: 7 items across 6 conventions.
- Blank (`______`, 6+ underscores) required in exactly one place for: words-in-context, inferences,
  boundaries, form-structure-sense, transitions. Forbidden everywhere else.

- Rhetorical-synthesis passages need ≥5 `•` bullets.
- Cross-text passages must contain literal `Text 1` and `Text 2`.
- Prose stimulus word count: ≥ skill floor (WIC 50 · TSP 75 · CTC 100 · CID 70 · COE 65 · INF 65 ·
  BND 35 · FSS 35 · TRN 45 · RS 70) and ≤150.
