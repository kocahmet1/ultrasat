## 3. Mathematical verification (`verify_M4.py`, sympy)

For each of the 22 items the script (a) recomputes the key from the stem's own premises, (b) re-derives
every distractor from the named recipe recorded in `_distractorLogic` and asserts it both **matches its
option string** and **differs from the key**, (c) confirms numeric option sets ascend, (d) validates every
`acceptedAnswers` entry, (e) audits blueprint quotas, the app format contract, and the rationale liturgy.

Representative re-derivations:

- **Q6 (function-notation nesting):** g(11) = 7, then f(7) = 2(7)² + 9 = 107; the script also proves
  f(11), g(11) and g(f(11)) are all different numbers, so the nesting is load-bearing.
- **Q8 (CB formula ladder, π-symbolic):** key π(5)²(12) = 300π; distractors are literally π(5)² = 25π
  (base area), π(5)(12) = 60π (radius not squared), 2π(5)(12) = 120π (lateral surface area).
- **Q10:** margins recomputed from the four cell counts (150 / 50 / 60 / 140 / 200) and asserted present in
  the rendered table; key 45/150 = 3/10; distractors 45/200 (grand-total denominator), 105/150 (row
  complement), 45/60 (transposed conditional).
- **Q17 (t vs t/n vs nt):** two structural moves, both machine-checked. `sympy.solve(m = 60t, t)` returns
  **t = m/60** (the inversion the stem no longer performs for the student), and 3(m/60) simplifies to
  **m/20**; `g(60t) = f(t)` is then verified symbolically at t = 0, 1, 5 and 7/3. The three wrong exponents
  m/60 (factor 3 dropped), 3m (no rescaling applied) and 180m (m = 60t misread as t = 60m) are each proved
  ≠ m/20 **and** proved not to coincide with the key at m = 60. The verifier now also asserts that the
  string `g(m) = f(m/60)` is **absent** from the stem — a standing guard against the round-1 regression.
- **Q18 (negative reciprocal):** (12, 20) confirmed on line k (12 + 4·20 = 92); k has slope −1/4, so the
  radius has slope 4; the centre (8, c) then forces c = 4, and the radius vector (4, 16) is proved
  orthogonal to k's direction vector (4, −1). The three distractors are re-derived from the opposite
  slope 1/4 (c = 19), the tangent slope −1/4 (c = 21) and the plain reciprocal −4 (c = 36).
- **Q20 (k vs k²):** perimeter ratio 4 = side ratio (proved symbolically for a general triangle), so the
  area ratio is 4² = 16 and 32(16) = 512; the three named errors give 32/16 = 2, 32/4 = 8 and 32(4) = 128.
- **Q21:** `sympy.solve(|x² − 4| = 3)` returns exactly 4 distinct real roots (±√7, ±1), each back-substituted.
- **Q22:** coefficient matching gives a = 3/2, b = 5; the identity is re-expanded and proved to hold for all x;
  ab = 15/2 confirmed in lowest terms.
- **Q16:** the 10 plotted points are proved strictly increasing, and a least-squares linear fit is proved to
  beat the exponential fit (SSE) with R² > 0.98 — so "Increasing linear" is unambiguous.
